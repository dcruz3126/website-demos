If you've lived somewhere else before moving to the Tampa area, you might have noticed your drains here seem to clog more often than they used to. That's not just in your head, a few things about Florida homes genuinely work against your pipes.

## Hard water builds up faster than you'd think

Much of Hillsborough County's water supply runs on the harder side, which means mineral deposits build up inside pipes gradually over time, narrowing the space water actually has to flow through. It's not something you can see happening, but it adds up over years, and it's part of why older homes in the area tend to have slower drains overall, even without an obvious clog.

## Grease behaves differently in warm kitchens

Pouring grease down the drain is always a bad idea, but in a warm kitchen, it stays liquid longer before it hits the cooler pipe further from the sink, which means it travels further before solidifying and catching other debris on its way. The fix is the same as anywhere: let grease cool in a container and toss it, don't rinse it down, even with hot water.

## Humidity affects more than just your hair

High humidity encourages organic buildup, everything from soap residue to skin cells, to break down more slowly in your pipes, which gives it more time to combine with hair and grime into an actual blockage instead of washing straight through.

## A few habits that genuinely help

- Run hot water for 15 to 20 seconds after using the kitchen sink, it helps keep grease moving before it has a chance to cool and stick.
- Use a simple mesh drain catcher in the shower. It's not glamorous, but hair is still one of the most common causes of slow bathroom drains, catcher or not.
- Skip the chemical drain cleaners as a routine habit. They can actually damage older pipes over repeated use, and they don't address buildup the way a proper cleaning does.
- If a drain is consistently slower than the others, that's usually a localized clog forming, worth addressing early rather than waiting for it to fully block.

None of this means your drains are doomed, it just means they're working a little harder than they would somewhere with softer water and lower humidity. A yearly check and clean goes a long way toward keeping things moving.
